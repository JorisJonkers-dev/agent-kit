# Lens: unasked questions

Check for choices the artifact makes silently.

- Look for a behaviour with several reasonable readings where the artifact
  picks one, or none, without saying who decided.
- Look for defaults, thresholds, names and locations that appear without a
  decision behind them.
- Look for a user story whose actor, trigger or outcome is assumed rather
  than stated.
- Phrase each finding as the question the owner should have been asked, with
  the quote that shows it was skipped.
