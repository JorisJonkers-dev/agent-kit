# Lens: fidelity

Check that the artifact says what was decided, all of it, and nothing more.

- Take each decision in the reference files and find where the artifact
  carries it out. A decision with no counterpart is an omission.
- Take each claim the artifact states as settled and find the decision behind
  it. A claim with none is an invention.
- Where both exist, compare them word for word. A changed number, a dropped
  condition, a "should" that became "may", or a narrowed scope is a
  contradiction.
- Check that domain terms carry the meaning the glossary gives them.
